Clayton Vault Backup

Privacy policy

Effective October 4, 2026

Clayton Vault Backup is operated by its owner for personal use. It is not offered as a service to other users.

Google account access

The utility uses Google OAuth to authorize rclone on the owner's computer. It requests Google Drive access to create, read, verify, and rotate encrypted backup archives in the owner's designated backup folder. This OAuth permission technically permits access to other Drive files; the configured backup job is limited to its backup folder.

The utility uses account identity and backup file metadata, including names, sizes, and checksums, to verify the destination and uploaded files. It may download encrypted archives to verify them.

Storage and protection

Local files are encrypted before they are uploaded to Google Drive. The decryption key is kept separately from the uploaded archives. OAuth credentials and tokens are stored in the owner's password manager or protected local configuration.

Backup retention is controlled by the owner. The configured policy keeps 7 daily backups and 4 weekly backups. Verification failures can postpone cleanup. Copies in Google Drive Trash remain subject to Google's retention rules.

Use and sharing

The utility uses Google user data only to perform and verify the owner's backups. It does not sell that data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Encrypted archives are stored with Google Drive. This public information website contains no backup files or credentials.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke the application's access in Google Account security settings, stop the local backup job, and delete backup files from Google Drive and its Trash. Revoking access does not automatically delete previously stored archives.

This website and contact

This site does not add analytics, forms, or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages, the hosting provider, may collect ordinary request logs under its own privacy policy. Questions may be sent to the app owner's support address shown on Google's consent screen.
